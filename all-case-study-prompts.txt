The scene shows systematic elimination of network components using packet evidence. Flat simple cartoon style, bold outlines, bright colours. Minimal text labels: "ARP OK", "DNS OK", "TCP SYN unanswered", "SMTP server crashed", "Network absolved", "smtp.packet-level.com".
